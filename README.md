## 👨‍💻 About

I'm a final-year **Diploma in ICT: Application Development** student at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology, graduating at the end of 2026. By day I work as an **IT field technician**, running Wi-Fi installs, fixing network outages and repairing hardware for homes and businesses. The rest of the time I build software.

Most of what I build is for someone specific: a business website running on the client's own domain, booking systems for local salons and practices, and team-built healthcare CRMs at university. I work across the stack, from **React and Next.js** frontends to **Spring Boot, Laravel and Supabase** backends, and I care about the parts users never see but always feel: authentication, data models, and booking flows that still work when real people use them.

I'm early in my career and I know it. What this profile shows is that I finish things, deploy them, and make each project better than the last.
