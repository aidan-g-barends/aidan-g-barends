## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a final-year **Diploma in ICT: Application Development** student at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology and a **freelance web developer** who builds full-stack apps for real clients. I've also spent time in the field as an **IT technician**, fixing real networks and hardware for homes and businesses, and I'm working toward becoming an **AI Engineer**.

Most of what I build is for someone specific: business websites running on the client's own domain, booking systems for local salons and practices, and team-built healthcare CRMs at university. I work across the stack, from **Next.js and React** frontends to **Spring Boot, Laravel and Supabase** backends, and I care about the parts users never see but always feel: authentication, data models, and booking flows that still work when real people use them.

My portfolio even has its own **AI assistant** that answers questions about my projects and experience. [Try it here](https://aidan-barends.vercel.app/#ask).
